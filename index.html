Pulsetto Fit — Neck Relaxation Device

A small device that sits on your neck and gives gentle pulses to help you relax, sleep better, and feel less stressed.

What you'll need

  • The Pulsetto Fit device (no charging needed the first time)
  • Your smartphone
  • The small tube of gel that came in the box

Setting it up, step by step

  1. Get the app. ⬇️ Download & Sign Up for Pulsetto
  2. Make an account. Open the app and follow the on-screen prompts. Just tap "Next" as you go.
  3. Turn on the device. In the app, tap the button to turn it on. A green light means it's ready to pair.
  4. Apply the gel. Put a small dab on your neck, right where you feel your pulse — a little goes a long way.
  5. Put the device on. Gently wrap it around your neck so it sits comfortably and securely.
  6. Pick a program and relax. In the app, choose Stress, Sleep, Burnout, or Pain Management. Start at the lowest intensity — you should feel a light tingle, not a jolt.
Good to know: Use it 2–3 times a day with a 45–60 minute break in between. To charge it, plug in the USB-C cable — it takes about an hour to fill up.

What to expect, from your first session on

Think of this like checking into a quiet spa treatment, not switching on a gadget. Here's what a typical welcome looks like:

  • A light tingling or gentle muscle flutter where the device sits — that's normal, and it settles once the pulses finish.
  • Many people notice a calmer, heavier feeling within the first few minutes, similar to the wind-down before a nap.
  • Sleep-focused sessions are best done 30–60 minutes before bed, as part of a wind-down routine rather than right at lights-out.
Please check with your doctor first if you: have a pacemaker, hearing aid implant, or any other implanted electronic device; have a metal plate, screw, or stent near your neck; are pregnant; have epilepsy or a seizure disorder; or have a heart condition. This is a wellness device, not a medical treatment, so it's worth a quick conversation with your doctor if any of that sounds like you.

What people say

Across independent reviews, the most common praise is a calmer mind within minutes of starting a session, and better sleep with regular use over a couple of weeks. The most common caution echoed by reviewers: results and sensations vary person to person, so starting at the lowest intensity and being patient for the first few sessions makes the best first impression.

Quick FAQ

  • Does it hurt? No — at the right intensity it should feel like a light tap or flutter, never sharp.
  • How long is a session? Sessions are guided by the app and typically run a few minutes to about 20, depending on the program chosen.
  • What if nothing happens? Double-check the gel is applied and the device is snug against the skin — a dry or loose fit is the most common reason for a "quiet" session.
  • Can I use it every day? Yes, 2–3 short sessions a day is the general guidance, with a rest of 45–60 minutes between them.
