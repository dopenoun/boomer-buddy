Wellness device

Pulsetto Fit

A plain-language setup guide for the wearable neck device and its companion phone app.

Before you start

  • The Pulsetto Fit device and its USB-C charging cable
  • The gel supplied with the device
  • An iPhone or Android phone with internet access and Bluetooth turned on
Safety comes first

Read Pulsetto’s current safety information before using the device. Do not use it without medical guidance if you have an implanted electronic or metal device near your neck, a seizure history, certain heart conditions, or if you are pregnant. Stop if use feels painful or unwell. See the manufacturer’s full safety list.

Set it up, step by step

  1. Get the Pulsetto Wellness app. Choose the store that matches your phone.
    Download for iPhone Download for Android
  2. Charge the device. Connect the USB-C cable and let the device charge before your first session.
  3. Open the app and sign in. Follow the prompts on the phone. Allow Bluetooth access if your phone asks.
  4. Use the supplied gel. Apply it exactly where the app’s current setup pictures show. The gel helps the device make good contact.
  5. Put the device on. Position it comfortably on your neck as shown in the app. It should be snug, not tight.
  6. Connect and choose a session. Follow the app to pair the device, then begin at the lowest comfortable intensity. Use the session length and rest time shown in the current app.

What to expect

  • A light tingling or gentle muscle movement can occur where the device touches your neck.
  • The feeling should not be sharp or painful. Lower the intensity or stop if it is uncomfortable.
  • Experiences vary. This consumer wellness device is not a replacement for medical care.

Quick answers

  • I do not feel anything. What should I check? Make sure the device is charged, the gel is applied, and both contact points sit against the skin. Then reconnect in the app.
  • The device will not connect. What now? Confirm Bluetooth and internet access are on, close and reopen the app, and keep the device near the phone.
  • How often should I use it? Follow the current schedule shown in the Pulsetto app or guidance from your healthcare professional.
  • Does it hurt? It should not feel sharp or painful. Stop the session if it does.
